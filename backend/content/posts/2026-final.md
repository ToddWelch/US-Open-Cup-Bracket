# St. Louis CITY SC Win the 2026 U.S. Open Cup on Penalties

St. Louis CITY SC won the 2026 Lamar Hunt U.S. Open Cup on October 21. They beat Columbus Crew 5-3 in a penalty shootout after 120 minutes without a goal at ScottsMiracle-Gro Field in Columbus, Ohio.

It's the first trophy in the club's history. The title is worth $600,000 in prize money and a place in the 2027 Concacaf Champions Cup.

## How the Final went

The match finished 0-0 after 90 minutes and 30 minutes of extra time, in front of 20,250 fans.

St. Louis captain Roman Bürki went off injured in the 78th minute. Backup goalkeeper Ben Lundt came on and saved the first Columbus penalty, taken by Santiago Rodríguez.

St. Louis scored all five of its kicks: Timo Baumgartl, Lukas MacNaughton, Chris Durkin, Damion Downs and Daniel Edelman. Columbus scored three, through Malte Amundsen, Gonzalo Tapia and Andrés Herrera.
