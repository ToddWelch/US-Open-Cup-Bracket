# Every Cupset of the 2026 U.S. Open Cup

A cupset is the Open Cup word for an upset: a team from a lower level of American soccer beating one from a higher level. The 2026 tournament had 12 of them.

The bracket ranks levels in this order: MLS, USL Championship, USL League One, MLS NEXT Pro, USL League Two, then other amateur leagues such as the UPSL and NPSL.

## How far the lower divisions got

Three non-MLS clubs reached the Round of 16, and all three went out there:

- Louisville City FC lost 2-1 after extra time at Houston Dynamo FC.
- Colorado Springs Switchbacks FC drew 2-2 at Colorado Rapids and lost on penalties.
- One Knoxville SC lost 4-1 at Columbus Crew.

All eight quarterfinalists were MLS clubs, and St. Louis CITY SC went on to win the title. The full bracket, with every cupset marked, is at [usopencup.welchproductsllc.com/2026](/2026).
